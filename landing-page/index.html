Place + career geography

Two bases.
One actual commute test.

Neither city is ranked. Test three plausible home addresses to the actual worksite, at the actual shift time, before choosing.

45.5229° N / 122.9898° W

Hillsboro

Best-supported geography for Intel and the dense west-side semiconductor ecosystem.

  • July 2026 all-home median: $529,235
  • Aug 2026 asking-rent orientation: $2,500 3BR / $3,106 4BR
  • MAX Blue and Red context; verify exact campus and shift.

Citywide orientation only. Not a family-home budget, appraisal, tax estimate, or availability claim.

45.4312° N / 122.7715° W

Tigard

Meaningful when the worksite is in Tualatin, including Lam Research facilities.

  • July 2026 all-home median: $599,675
  • Aug 2026 asking-rent orientation: $2,595 3BR / $3,700 4BR
  • WES is weekday rush-hour service; test Intel commutes directly.

School assignment is address-specific; a Tigard mailing address does not prove district assignment.

The employment gate

Job first. Property second.

The strongest bridge is senior technical program, engineering operations, systems architecture, or AI product/solutions work. Intel is a documented Hillsboro anchor; Lam supports the Tigard–Tualatin geography. NVIDIA is a role-by-role lead only. A written offer or durable remote arrangement—not an assumed opening—unlocks a binding housing commitment.
