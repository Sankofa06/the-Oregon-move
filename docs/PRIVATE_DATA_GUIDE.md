# Private Planning Data Guide

The public repository and Pages site are the shared framework. The household numbers are private working data.

## Safe workflow

1. Open the published planner and choose **Start a private plan**.
2. Enter only financial estimates—never names, addresses, account numbers, loan numbers, credentials, or resume contact information.
3. Leave **Save on this device** off on a shared or public computer.
4. Use **Hide values** before screen sharing.
5. Export only when a second device needs the populated plan. The JSON export is unencrypted; share it through a private channel, store it carefully, and delete copies that are no longer needed.
6. Never commit an export. Files matching `oregon-move-private-plan-*.json` and `*.private.json` are ignored, but the final responsibility remains with the person committing.

## Household baseline to gather

- Monthly take-home income for each reliable source and the date any expected change begins.
- Every cash balance, pending transaction, and restricted or earmarked amount.
- Essential monthly spending: housing, utilities, childcare, insurance, food, transportation, and pet care.
- Each debt's balance, minimum, rate, due date, status, payoff/cure amount, and any active agreement.
- Credit reports/scores, utilization, recent inquiries, and any disputed or derogatory items.
- Tax payment arrangements, unread notices, and retirement-distribution documents requiring professional review.
- A household-selected minimum reserve in dollars and months.

## Current-home sale packet

- Mortgage and HELOC payoff statements.
- Expected sale range from current evidence, not an old online estimate.
- Negotiated commission assumption, seller fees/taxes, concessions, and fixed closing costs.
- Repair/staging quotes and a list of projects likely to improve net proceeds or sale certainty.
- Title/ownership, lien, permit, insurance-claim, basis, and capital-improvement records.
- Two seller net sheets and the maximum tolerable housing overlap.

## Oregon housing packet

For every live option, record the source and date for price/rent, tax, insurance, school assignment, commute, pet rules, condition, utilities, fees, and closing/move-in timing.

Land/build requires additional written evidence: zoning and overlays, legal access, wetland/flood/slope screening, utility availability and capacity, well/septic where applicable, survey/geotechnical needs, jurisdiction fees, builder bids, construction-finance terms, carrying cost, and a rent-first fallback.

## Career packet

- Target role family and why the experience is a truthful fit.
- Employer, exact worksite or remote terms, schedule/shift, compensation components, relocation support, application stage, and next action.
- Interview stories covering systems leadership, automation, regulated execution, supplier/customer collaboration, AI-assisted products, and executive decision support.
- Explicit gaps that must not be overstated, especially deep fabrication, silicon, low-level GPU, hyperscale infrastructure, or research experience.
