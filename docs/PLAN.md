# Oregon Move Decision Plan

**Working version:** 2.0 — September 2, 2026

**Physical move target:** August 21–27, 2027

**School-ready checkpoint:** August 30, 2027

**Family-settled milestone:** September 1, 2027

**Current phase:** establish a verified household baseline, current-home exit range, and qualified job pipeline.

## The next decision

Do not choose a property yet. First validate:

1. usable move funds after the current-home sale, planned debt payoff, moving costs, cash to close, and reserve preservation;
2. the resulting monthly cash flow after debt payments are eliminated; and
3. each likely job's actual worksite, schedule, and durable remote expectations.

Those facts determine the sensible area and housing path. A purchase or land commitment waits for documented employment, lender orientation, a current-home net sheet, property-specific diligence, and a fallback for timing delays.

## Financial model

Use low, working, and stretch sale cases without treating any automated value as an appraisal:

`net sale = sale price − mortgage payoff − other liens − variable selling costs − preparation − fixed closing costs − concessions`

`usable move funds = liquid funds + other move funds + net sale − planned debt payoff − moving/travel`

Debt payoff is deducted once from available cash. The related eliminated monthly payment is credited once to ongoing non-housing cash flow. Keep those two effects separate.

The affordability panel uses household gross income, monthly debt obligations, estimated housing costs, a household-selected comfort ceiling, and a purchase-price ceiling. Its front-end and back-end debt-to-income percentages are screening guides only. A lender must replace them with an actual preapproval and Loan Estimate.

## Four housing paths

### Rent first

The flexible bridge when job geography, sale timing, credit readiness, or land feasibility is unresolved. Compare current dog-compatible rentals with the needed bedrooms, written pet terms, exact school assignment, air conditioning/air-quality needs, parking, and real commute tests. Budget for moving twice.

### Move-in-ready purchase

The direct ownership path when employment, financing, sale proceeds, and dates align. Replace medians with actual comparable homes, Loan Estimates, parcel tax statements, insurance quotes, inspections, HOA documents, and seller disclosures.

### Fixer purchase

Viable only if the home is safely habitable and the verified work fits inside the all-in ceiling:

`maximum offer = move-in-ready comparable − immediate work − contingency − carrying/overlap`

Inspect roof, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, sewer, permits, insurance eligibility, and lead risk for older homes. A generic “fixer discount” is not evidence.

### Land and build

The highest-uncertainty path and likely a two-stage move. Model land, access, utilities/well/septic, survey/soil/geotechnical work, design, permits and system-development charges, site work, construction, financing carry, rent overlap, and contingency separately. No land closing without written jurisdiction feasibility, insurability, multiple bids, and a rent-first fallback.

The map is a real geographic pan-and-zoom view backed by OpenStreetMap tiles, but it remains a screening tool rather than a route engine. Its commute labels are qualitative geographic inferences; actual traffic, shift time, and campus can change the answer. Open the selected area or area-to-employer directions in Apple Maps to examine the road network, then test finalist trips at the actual shift time.

- **Two-career compromise:** Beaverton and Cedar Mill/Bethany offer useful access to Nike, Intel, and Portland professional-services work.

For every serious address, verify school assignment, flood/wetland/slope exposure, insurance, taxes, utilities, pet fit, and employer commute at likely work times. “Near water” is not the same as a view, legal access, or low flood risk.

## 12-month roadmap

The live Gantt is the source of truth for dates, owners, durations, and status. Its starting structure is:

- **Sep–Oct 2026:** reconcile the financial baseline; set family must-haves; refresh career tracks.
- **Nov–Dec 2026:** collect payoffs, repair quotes, valuation views, and seller net sheets; build matched rental and purchase sets.
- **Jan–Feb 2027:** scout areas, test real commute windows, verify schools by address, and pass the first career gate.
- **Mar–Apr 2027:** choose a primary/backup area and housing path; refresh rates, taxes, insurance, and parcel screens.
- **May–Jun 2027:** pass income and current-home exit gates; complete sale-positive preparation; decide the closing/lease sequence.
- **Jul 2027:** secure housing only after property- or lease-specific diligence and retain backup lodging/storage.
- **Aug 21–27, 2027:** execute the move with timing buffers.
- **Aug 30, 2027:** school-ready checkpoint before district classes begin.
- **Sep 1, 2027:** family-settled milestone and second K–12 attendance day in both current target districts.

The adopted 2027–28 calendars for both current target districts start many K–12 grades on **August 31, 2027**. Hillsboro starts its other K–12 grades on September 1. Tigard-Tualatin has all grades 1–12 attending September 1 and lists Pre-K/Kindergarten beginning September 8. Preschool enrollment, placement, and dates remain program-specific. Recheck calendars, boundaries, and enrollment rules before the move because revisions can occur.

## Review rhythm

- **Weekly while applying:** openings, exact locations, schedules, compensation, and remote terms.
- **Monthly:** current-home range, matched listings/rents, mortgage benchmark, and model assumptions.
- **Quarterly:** full balance sheet, credit readiness, reserve floor, tax questions, and target-date viability.
- **Every property or lease:** title/access, parcel tax, zoning/overlays, insurance, inspection, school, commute, pet terms, utilities, hazards, and written fees.
