## The decision in one page

The family is exploring Hillsboro and Tigard while pursuing senior engineering, technical-program, product, operations, or AI-solutions work in Oregon's semiconductor and adjacent technology ecosystem. Intel is the strongest documented Hillsboro anchor; Lam Research makes the Tigard–Tualatin corridor a meaningful second geography. NVIDIA remains a role-by-role target, not a relocation premise.

Do not select a property yet. The next decision is to validate two things:

1. the true move cash available after current obligations, reserve needs, and a conservative home-sale net; and
2. the likely worksite, shift pattern, or durable remote-work arrangement.

Both determine which town and housing path are sensible.

## Hard move gates

A purchase, land closing, or construction commitment waits until all applicable gates are satisfied:

- **Financial baseline:** balances, debt status, income, monthly essentials, credit readiness, tax/retirement questions, and reserve floor are current and documented.
- **Employment:** signed offer, verified durable remote arrangement, or another conservative income plan supports the move.
- **Current-home exit:** payoff, likely sale range, repair plan, seller costs, tax questions, and overlap exposure have written estimates.
- **Housing proof:** property- or lease-specific tax, insurance, school, commute, pet, title, inspection, and permit facts replace city averages.
- **Fallback:** temporary housing and timing buffers exist if the sale, closing, job start, or build moves.

## The four paths

### Rent first

The schedule-flexible bridge when job geography, sale timing, credit readiness, or a buildable parcel is unresolved. Compare at least ten current dog-compatible listings with enough bedrooms, written pet terms, address-based school assignment, HVAC/air quality, parking, and exact commute tests. Budget for the possibility of moving twice.

### Move-in-ready purchase

The most direct ownership path when employment, financing, sale proceeds, and closing dates align. Replace median prices with actual comparable properties, Loan Estimates, parcel tax statements, insurance quotes, inspection findings, HOA documents, and a seller net sheet.

### Fixer purchase

Viable only if the home is safely habitable and the verified work fits inside the all-in cap. A conservative maximum-offer frame is:

`move-in-ready comparable cap − immediate work − contingency − carrying/overlap = maximum offer`

Inspect roof, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, sewer, permit history, insurance eligibility, and lead risk for most pre-1978 homes. A generic “fixer discount” is not evidence.

### Land and build

The highest-uncertainty and likely two-stage path. Model land, acquisition, survey/soil/geotechnical work, design and engineering, permits and system-development charges, access/site work, utilities or well/septic, vertical construction, financing carry, rent overlap, and contingency separately.

No land purchase proceeds without written jurisdiction feasibility, legal access, utility evidence, flood/wetland and slope screening, septic approval where applicable, an insurance indication, and multiple builder bids. For this schedule, “move to Oregon first, finish the custom home later” is the explicit fallback.

## Place strategy

### Hillsboro

- Best-supported employment geography for Intel and a dense semiconductor supplier/customer-support ecosystem.
- Blue and Red MAX service plus a current bus connection to Intel Ronler Acres.
- The July 2026 all-home-type median sale price and August 2026 asking-rent references belong in the planner as dated orientation points, not a family-home budget.
- Exact campus, school address, parcel taxes, and commute still decide fit.

### Tigard

- Stronger geographic fit when the worksite is in Tualatin, including Lam Research facilities.
- WES connects Tigard with Tualatin and Beaverton during weekday rush periods; it is not all-day service.
- The July/August 2026 sale and rent references are similarly directional.
- An Intel/Hillsboro commute needs an exact shift-time test, and a Tigard mailing address does not by itself prove school-district assignment.

Neither city is ranked. The practical test is three plausible home locations to the actual worksite at the actual shift time.

- AI product, platform, or solutions leadership;
- systems architecture and digital engineering; and

Deep semiconductor process/yield, silicon reliability, GPU kernel/driver, hyperscale-cluster ownership, and deep-learning research roles need evidence the current career record does not yet support. The search should emphasize transferable systems leadership without claiming those specialties.

## Current-home sale model

Use three sale cases—conservative, working, and stretch—without treating any as an appraisal.

`net sale = sale price − mortgage/HELOC payoffs − negotiated commissions − seller fees/taxes − preparation/repairs − concessions`

Gather current payoff statements, title and ownership documents, liens, basis and improvement records, permits/claims, two comparative market analyses or another valuation opinion, two seller net sheets, and a sale-positive repair plan. Decide sell-first, contingent purchase, rent-back, or temporary housing only after modeling the maximum overlap the household can carry.

## Review rhythm

- **Weekly once applying:** employer openings and work-location requirements.
- **Monthly:** purchase medians, filtered rent comps, mortgage benchmark, sale estimate, and the move dashboard.
- **Quarterly:** full household balance sheet, credit readiness, tax/retirement questions, and plan/date viability.
- **For every actual address:** tax statement, insurance, zoning/overlays, title/access, school assignment, commute, pet restrictions, and inspections.
