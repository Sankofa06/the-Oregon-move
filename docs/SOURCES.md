# Evidence Registry

**Accessed:** September 2, 2026 unless otherwise shown.

**Rule:** medians, automated valuations, maps, and commute-fit labels are orientation inputs—not appraisals, route guarantees, offers, leases, tax bills, school assignments, or proof that a parcel works.

## Current-home sale research

- [Pierce County parcel and property information](https://www.piercecountywa.gov/969/Parcel-Property-Information) — official entry point for parcel, assessed-value, and tax facts. An assessment is not expected sale price.
- [Redfin home-value entry point](https://www.redfin.com/what-is-my-home-worth) — automated values are only range inputs. Replace them with a local comparative market analysis and two seller net sheets.
- [Freddie Mac — costs of selling](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/selling/costs-of-selling) — broad cost categories; commissions are negotiable and local written estimates control.
- [IRS Topic 701](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc701) and [Publication 523](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p523) — general main-home sale rules. Use a tax professional for the actual ownership, use, gain, and reporting facts.

## Financing and taxes

- [Freddie Mac — mortgage rates and affordability](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/buying/mortgage-rates) — the August 27, 2026 national 30-year fixed benchmark was 6.66%; it is not a household quote or 2027 forecast.
- [CFPB — determine your down payment](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/prepare/determine-your-down-payment/) — use Loan Estimates for actual cash to close and mortgage insurance.
- [CFPB — decide how much to spend](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/prepare/figure-out-how-much-you-want-to-spend/) — preserve a household-selected cushion after moving and renovation costs.
- [Oregon Department of Revenue — property tax](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/property/pages/personal-property.aspx) — Oregon tax depends on assessed value and district rates, not sale price alone.
- [Oregon personal income tax](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/individuals/pages/pit.aspx) and [Portland-area personal taxes](https://www.portland.gov/revenue/personal-tax) — refresh thresholds and work/home jurisdiction with a tax professional.

## Water, hazards, and land/build

- [Oregon HazVu](https://www.oregon.gov/dogami/hazvu/pages/index.aspx) — statewide flood, landslide, earthquake, and other geohazard screening; not a survey or guarantee.
- [Oregon OEM flood maps](https://www.oregon.gov/oem/emresources/Plans_Assessments/riskmap/Pages/Flood-Maps.aspx) — water-oriented candidates need address-specific flood, wetland, bank, access, and insurance diligence.
- [Washington County GIS](https://www.washingtoncountyor.gov/gis) — parcel orientation; obtain written jurisdiction, zoning, utility, and buildability evidence.
- [Oregon well-log query](https://apps.wrd.state.or.us/apps/gw/well_log/default.aspx) — a nearby log does not prove water capacity or quality.
- [Oregon DEQ onsite septic systems](https://www.oregon.gov/deq/residential/pages/onsite-about.aspx) — an unsewered parcel requires site evaluation; approval is not guaranteed.
- [Oregon wetlands and waters maps](https://www.oregon.gov/dsl/wetlands-waters/pages/inventories-maps.aspx) — approximate screening boundaries can omit wetlands.
- [Oregon wildfire and insurance](https://dfr.oregon.gov/insure/home/storm/pages/wildfires.aspx) — obtain a property-specific insurance indication before committing.

## Refresh cadence

- **Weekly during active job search:** openings, location, schedule, compensation, and remote-work terms.
- **Monthly:** current-home range, filtered listings/rentals, mortgage benchmark, and model assumptions.
- **January 2027 and before commitment:** taxes, permit fees, insurance, lender assumptions, school calendars, and transit.
- **Every property or lease:** tax, title/access, zoning/overlays, hazards, insurance, inspection, school assignment, commute, pet terms, utilities, and written fees.
