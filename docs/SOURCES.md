## Housing, rent, and financing orientation

- [Redfin — Tigard housing market](https://www.redfin.com/city/18733/OR/Tigard/housing-market) — July 2026 all-home-type median sale price used by the planner: approximately $599,675.
- [Zillow Rental Manager — Hillsboro](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/hillsboro-or/) — updated August 31, 2026; asking-rent averages include approximately $2,500 for three bedrooms and $3,106 for four bedrooms.
- [Zillow Rental Manager — Tigard](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/tigard-or/) — updated August 27, 2026; asking-rent averages include approximately $2,595 for three bedrooms and $3,700 for four bedrooms.
- Rental vendors use different listing mixes and statistics. Do not average them. Replace citywide data with at least ten current dog-compatible, bedroom-matched listings 60–90 days before moving.
- [Freddie Mac — Mortgage rates and affordability](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/buying/mortgage-rates) — August 27, 2026 national 30-year fixed benchmark used by the reference case: 6.66%. It is not an individual quote or a 2027 forecast.
- [CFPB — Determine your down payment](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/prepare/determine-your-down-payment/) — closing-cost orientation of roughly 2–5% excluding down payment; replace with lender Loan Estimates.
- [CFPB — Figure out how much you want to spend](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/prepare/figure-out-how-much-you-want-to-spend/) — discusses retaining an emergency cushion after moving, renovation, and furnishing costs. The household selects its own floor.
- [Freddie Mac — Costs of selling](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/selling/costs-of-selling) — broad seller-cost categories and ranges; commissions are negotiable and local net sheets control.

## Taxes

- [Oregon Department of Revenue — Property tax](https://www.oregon.gov/DOR/programs/property/Pages/personal-property.aspx) — Oregon property tax uses assessed value and district rates; a sale price or bare-land bill is not a finished-home tax estimate.
- [Oregon Department of Revenue — Personal income tax](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/individuals/Pages/PIT.aspx) and [withholding](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/individuals/pages/pit-withholding.aspx) — refresh brackets, deductions, and withholding for 2027; do not apply the top marginal rate as a flat rate.
- [Oregon Secretary of State — State revenue](https://sos.oregon.gov/blue-book/explore/Pages/economy-revenue.aspx) — Oregon has no general state sales tax.
- [City of Portland Revenue — Personal taxes](https://www.portland.gov/revenue/personal-tax) — Metro Supportive Housing Services tax may apply above indexed thresholds. Multnomah County and City of Portland taxes are separate; verify home and work jurisdiction.
- [TriMet — Payroll and self-employment taxes](https://trimet.org/taxinfo/) — refresh the statewide transit-tax and employer/self-employed rules with payroll/tax professionals.

## Land, build, and fixer diligence

- [Hillsboro — Permitting rates and fees](https://www.hillsboro-oregon.gov/services/permitting-center/rates-fees) — zoning/development review, building, site/erosion, utilities, and system-development charges can all apply; obtain a project-specific 2027 estimate.
- [Tigard — System development charges](https://www.tigard-or.gov/your-government/departments/community-development/permit-center/system-development-charges-sdc) — transportation, parks, water, sewer/stormwater, and county charges may apply and update annually.
- [Oregon DEQ — Onsite septic systems](https://www.oregon.gov/deq/Residential/Pages/Onsite-about.aspx) — an undeveloped unsewered parcel needs a site evaluation; approval is not guaranteed.
- [Oregon DSL — Wetlands and waters maps](https://www.oregon.gov/dsl/wetlands-waters/Pages/inventories-maps.aspx) — maps are screening tools with approximate boundaries and possible unmapped wetlands, not buildability proof.
- [Tigard — Zoning](https://www.tigard-or.gov/your-government/departments/community-development/planning/zoning) and [Hillsboro — Maps/data](https://www.hillsboro-oregon.gov/our-city/departments/community-development/maps-data-resources) — check base zoning plus flood, wetland, slope, resource, and other overlays for the actual parcel.
- [Washington County — Flood information](https://www.washingtoncountyor.gov/emergency/flood) — use the FEMA address-based map and obtain a property-specific insurance quote.
- [Oregon Division of Financial Regulation — Wildfire and insurance](https://dfr.oregon.gov/insure/home/storm/pages/wildfires.aspx) — insurers use property-specific underwriting; obtain an indication before committing.
- [EPA — Lead-based paint disclosure rule](https://www.epa.gov/lead/lead-based-paint-disclosure-rule-section-1018-title-x) — most pre-1978 housing triggers disclosure and inspection considerations; fixer work needs a lead-safe plan.

## Refresh cadence

- **Weekly during the active search:** openings, location, schedule, compensation, and remote-work terms.
- **Monthly:** sale medians, dog-compatible rent comps, mortgage benchmark, and current-home sale range.
- **January 2027 and immediately before commitment:** tax thresholds, permit/SDC schedules, insurance, lender assumptions, school calendars, and transit schedules.
- **Every property or lease:** parcel tax, title/access, zoning/overlays, insurance, inspection, school assignment, commute, pet terms, utilities, and written fees.
